# Russian IP Compliance (ИП)

## Tax Regimes

### УСН (Упрощённая система налогообложения)
- **Доходы** (6%): simplest, pay 6% of revenue
- **Доходы минус расходы** (15%): need to track expenses
- Income limit: 150M rubles (2026)
- Must issue KKT for cash payments

### ПСН (Патентная система)
- Fixed payment instead of taxes
- Limited by types of activities and regions
- Revenue limit: 60M rubles (2026)

### НПД (Налог на профессиональный доход)
- For individuals, not ИП
- 4% for individuals, 6% for professionals
- Limit: 10M rubles (2026)

## KKT Requirements (54-ФЗ)
- Mandatory online cash registers since 2018
- Must print fiscal receipt or send fiscal signal
- Online KKT required — no offline devices
- Check codes mandatory
- Data stored 3+ years
- Fines: 10k-30k rubles for first violation

## 152-ФЗ for ИП
- ИП is an operator if processes personal data
- Must have: Privacy Policy, consent, order appointing responsible
- Consent must be separate document since 09.2025
- Cross-border transfer restrictions apply
- Fines: Art. 13.11 КоАП — up to 100k rubles

## Safety Requirements
- Mandatory workplace safety (ОТ)
- Fire safety (ПБ)
- If selling BADs: SanPiN, TR CU 021/2011, TR CU 022/2011
- Mandatory medical examinations for certain activities
- Employer liability for workplace safety violations

## Website Requirements for ИП
- Must display: INN, OGRNIP, name, address, bank details
- Must have Privacy Policy (152-ФЗ)
- If collecting PD: separate consent document
- No foreign analytics without localization
- For e-commerce: 54-ФЗ compliance for all payments

## Forbidden for ИП
- No foreign bank accounts for business (since 2021)
- No foreign payment systems for Russian clients
- No anonymous payments
- Must use Russian domain for certain activities
