# Russian Compliance for Physical Persons (ФЛ) and ИП

## For Physical Persons (ФЛ) — Non-Entrepreneur

### Can a ФЛ Run a Website/Sell Goods?
- Yes, but with restrictions
- Selling goods in large quantities without registration = potential entrepreneurship risk
- NPD (Налог на профессиональный доход) available since 2019 — 4% for ФЛ, 6% for self-employed professionals
- NPD limit: 10M rubles (2026)
- If revenue exceeds limits or activity looks entrepreneurial — may need to register as ИП

### Taxes for ФЛ
- НДФЛ (13%): on income from entrepreneurial activity if not using NPD
- NPD: 4% for physical persons, 6% for professionals (lawyers, notaries, etc.)
- Must register for NPD via "Налогоплательщик" app or tax service
- No KKT required for NPD (simplified)

### 152-ФЗ for ФЛ
- ФЛ is an operator if processes personal data commercially
- Must have: Privacy Policy, consent to process PD
- Consent must be separate document since 09.2025
- Cross-border transfer restrictions apply
- Fines: Art. 13.11 КоАП — up to 100k rubles

### Website Requirements for ФЛ
- Privacy Policy required if collecting PD (152-ФЗ)
- No INN/OGRNIP required (only for ИП)
- Must not engage in unregistered entrepreneurial activity
- No foreign payment systems for Russian clients (if commercial)
- Russian domain recommended for certain activities

### Prohibited for ФЛ
- Selling goods in large volumes without tax registration (risk of ИП status)
- Foreign bank accounts for business income (since 2021)
- Anonymous commercial payments
- Certain activities require ИП or legal entity (pharmacy, alcohol, etc.)

## For ИП (Individual Entrepreneur)

### Tax Regimes
- **УСН "Доходы"** (6%): simplest, pay 6% of revenue
- **УСН "Доходы минус расходы"** (15%): need to track expenses
- Income limit: 150M rubles (2026)
- **ПСН**: fixed payment, limited by region/activity, revenue limit 60M rubles (2026)

### KKT Requirements (54-ФЗ)
- Mandatory online cash registers since 2018
- Online KKT required — no offline devices
- Fines: 10k-30k rubles first violation

### Must Display on Website (for ИП)
- INN, OGRNIP, name, address, bank details
- Privacy Policy with ИП as operator

### Safety Requirements
- Workplace safety (ОТ), fire safety (ПБ)
- If selling BADs: SanPiN, TR CU 021/2011, TR CU 022/2011
